ONGOING RESEARCH

Computational Investigation of Para-Haloacetophenone Derivatives

This research focuses on the computational investigation of three para-haloacetophenone derivatives to explore their molecular structures, electronic characteristics, and chemical reactivity.

Target Compounds: 1. 4-Chloroacetophenone • 2. 4-Bromoacetophenone • 3. 4-Fluoroacetophenone
Research Analysis: FMO Analysis • NBO Analysis • NLO Analysis • TDM Analysis • DOS Analysis • Global Reactivity Parameters
Research Objective: To comparatively investigate the computational behavior and electronic characteristics of these molecules and understand how different halogen substituents influence their molecular properties.
